Somewhere on the internet, I found [40 questions](https://stephango.com/40-questions) to ask yourself at the end of each year. So here I am, rewinding 2025 — one question at a time.

---

**1. What did you do this year that you'd never done before?**

Volunteered at Google DevFest. I would have never done it alone, but I made a decision, met new people, and had a wonderful time. Seems it's not that hard to be out. 🎉

**2. Did you keep your new year's resolutions?**

No. I don't believe in taking one and dropping it. I strongly believe every turning point comes at different times and places. Forcing life on a new year is not recommended.

**3. Did anyone close to you give birth?**

No.

**4. Did anyone close to you die?**

Yes. (Prefer not to discuss.)

**5. What cities/states/countries did you visit?**

Dubai, for work. One country at my best. 🇦🇪

**6. What would you like to have next year that you lacked this year?**

More courage, peace of mind, planning, and happiness.

**7. What date(s) from this year will remain etched upon your memory, and why?**

April 20. Got my job after a very long time. Something amazing — being in a new place and culture.

**8. What was your biggest achievement of the year?**

I'm a laid-back person. This year, my biggest achievement was that I started to think better. I have clarity. I'm choosing peace and happiness over everything in my life — that feels like a big achievement. ✨

**9. What was your biggest failure?**

My biggest failure is that I did not stand up for myself — not courageous or bold enough to stand up for my peace and happiness. I have a lot of complaints from myself to myself. 😅

**10. What other hardships did you face?**

Being in a new place, adapting to a new culture personally and professionally, took a toll on my mind and heart. Everything new was really hard for me as an introvert — new people, new country, new place, new smile, new fear.

**11. Did you suffer illness or injury?**

Not much, but here and there. Nothing serious.

**12. What was the best thing you bought?**

AI tool subscriptions. The best, *bestest* thing I have ever got. I feel like a superhero now. 🦸

**13. Whose behavior merited celebration?**

I'm not mature enough to gauge something like this yet.

**14. Whose behavior made you appalled?**

Same — I'm not mature enough to judge.

**15. Where did most of your money go?**

Investments, getting things I wished to get as my 10, 20, 30-year-old self. 😂 Even though I'm an adult, I'm planning a lot with my money. Still learning.

**16. What did you get really, really, really excited about?**

AI tool subscriptions. I'm really really really really really excited about using them daily. I'm just on cloud nine. ☁️

**17. What song will always remind you of this year?**

"Ularal" from *Kudumbasthanm*, sung by Ravi G. 🎵 [Hear the song →](https://youtu.be/3PvIKlVf-5k)

**18. Compared to this time last year, are you: happier or sadder? Richer or poorer? Healthier or unhealthier?**

Compared to last year, I feel free. Like freedom of a bird. Mentally, I'm healthier, marching toward happiness and peace. I'm richer in understanding, clarity, peace of mind, happiness, and life — and money too, which has been a great tool that led me here. 🕊️

**19. What do you wish you'd done more of?**

Physical activity and movement.

**20. What do you wish you'd done less of?**

Sleeping. This year, I wasted most of my time sleeping. I slept a lot. 😴

**21. How are you spending the holidays?**

Mostly occupied or doing something on my laptop. 💻

**22. Did you fall in love this year?**

Yes, I fell in love with myself after a long breakup. 💕

**23. Do you hate anyone now that you didn't hate this time last year?**

A lot of people, but that's subjective. It might just change anytime, or most probably I would forget all of them.

**24. What was your favorite show?**

Not much of a show watcher.

**25. What was the best book you read?**

*Thinking, Fast and Slow.*

**26. What was your greatest musical discovery of the year?**

I love songs. Every song I listened to was great. The albums from *Dude*, *F1*, and *Madarasi* were great.

**27. What was your favorite film?**

*Kudumbasthanm*. I've watched it 1, 5, 10, like infinite times. ♾️ [Watch the trailer →](https://youtu.be/qfKpPq87bHQ)

**28. What was your favorite meal?**

Paruppu sadam (lentil rice) and aavakai oorugai (mango pickle). 🍚

**29. What did you want and get?**

A job.

**30. What did you want and not get?**

Nothing really. Mostly had everything I wished for this year.

**31. What did you do on your birthday?**

Went to the temple, had a good, nice day at home. 🎂

**32. What one thing would have made your year immeasurably more satisfying?**

My health. It was really bad this year. I had to work a lot on that — the lack of food and movement.

**33. How would you describe your personal fashion this year?**

Worst fashion sense of a duck. White shirt, orange pants. 🦆

**34. What kept you sane?**

Yesterday, today, tomorrow, and till my life ends — movies. I watch them every day. No doubt. My home, my heart, my mind — everything is who I am because of movies. Learning happiness and sadness. It's always movies. 🎬

**35. Which celebrity/public figure did you admire the most?**

I really admire Vijay, Tamil actor turned politician. Just my thought, though. It's a great move from where he is.

**36. What political issue stirred you the most?**

I'm not much of a politically-following person. My views are mostly from the public side. Maybe I haven't seen the rabbit hole.

**37. Who did you miss?**

To be honest, I missed everyone. My old office, college, friends, and family. It's been a rollercoaster year. 🎢

**38. Who was the best new person you met?**

Afroz. One of the kindest, softest, calmest, most energetic people I have ever seen. I feel blessed to have such a person in my life. 🙏

**39. What valuable life lesson did you learn this year?**

To be more humble, patient, and set boundaries.

**40. What is a quote that sums up your year?**

"Human-being-Human" sums up my year — ups and downs. We are human beings, understood, but we have to be humane. The most important part of my life.
